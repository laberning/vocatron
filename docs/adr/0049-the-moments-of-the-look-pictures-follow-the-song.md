# ADR-0049: The pictures of the looks show a line sung together, in song order

Supersedes ADR-0045 in part: which moment the second and third pictures of a sheet show. Everything
else of ADR-0045 stays as it is.

## Context and problem

ADR-0045 shows "a line being sung" as the first line of the second singer, half sung, and "a line
change" just after the look's first change once both singers sing together. The second singer's
first line was not a good part of the song to show. On the English demo duet the change moment also
came before the sung moment in most looks, so a sheet did not read in song order.

## Considered options

1. **The first line sung together, and the first change after it.** The second picture is the middle
   of the first line sung by a group or by all; the third is the look's first change after that.
2. **A line where the singer changes in the middle of the line.** It shows two singers' colours in one
   line, but the demo duet has no such line.
3. **Keep the second singer's first line** and only fix the order.

## Decision

Option 1.

- "A line sung together": the middle of the first line sung by a group or by all. Without one, the
  first line of a singer other than the first line's; with one singer, the first line.
- "A line change": the look's first change after that moment; if none, the first line that appears
  after it; if no line appears, the sung line itself.
- The six moments read in song order: start, sung together, change, break, countdown, end.
- The overview shows the moment "a line sung together".

## Consequences

- The sheets show the group colour, and in the stacked and page looks the solo line before it.
- The rule follows the demo song: a new demo song needs no new choice of moments.
